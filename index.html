Program Guide
Oura Readiness Protocol · All Three Days
85+
Full Send
Execute the session exactly as written. This is your day to add load — if all sets felt RPE 7 or below last session, add 5 lb to barbells. Progressive overload happens on green days.
70–84
Modified
Primary compounds only. Skip supersets and finishers. Keep post-session restorative. Don't add load — maintain what you built. Still a productive session.
<70
Recovery Day
No lifting. 20–30 min easy walk + 20 min restorative yoga. Your nervous system is under load — adding more suppresses the HRV you're trying to build. Rest is the training today.
Flexible Scheduling Rules
  • Never lift two days back to back — any combination of A, B, or C
  • Day A, B, and C can run in any order based on your week
  • A heavy BBC upper body day counts as a buffer — don't run Day B or C the following day
  • If you only get two sessions in a week, prioritize A and B — Day C is the first to drop
  • Oura Readiness governs load on all three days — no exceptions
12-Week Progression
WKS 1–4
Foundation
Learn your numbers. Keep RPE at 7/10 maximum. Add 5 lb to barbell lifts only when all sets feel like RPE 7 or below with 1–2 reps in reserve. Log every session. Check Oura daily. Prioritize the post-session restorative — this is where you build your HRV baseline. Goal: Readiness consistently above 75.
WKS 5–8
Build
Add tempo work — 3-count eccentric, 1-sec pause, 1-count concentric on hip thrusts and curls. Increase barbell loads 5–10 lb where form is solid. Introduce drop sets on the B1/B2 superset final round and S2 on Day C. Goal: Oura Readiness averaging 80+ consistently.
WKS 9–12
Define
Reduce rest periods to 60 sec on all accessory work. Full drop sets on hip thrusts, curls, lateral raises. Test near-max on barbell hip thrust and RDL — this is your benchmark. Re-scan InBody at week 12 to measure skeletal muscle change and assess asymmetry progress.
